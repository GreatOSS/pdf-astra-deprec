A little help

Read: select and copy text with Select. Use the page list or arrows to navigate. Fit width adapts to your screen.

Annotate: choose Add text, enter your text, then click the page. Drag a rectangle to highlight. Choose a PNG or JPEG, then click to place it. In Select mode, click an added item to remove it using Delete or Backspace.

Organize: rotate, move or delete the current page. Every edit supports undo and redo.

Keep: Export PDF downloads a copy. Your original file is never overwritten. The last session is stored locally in this browser; Close & forget removes it.

Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ Z to undo, Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z to redo, Ctrl/⌘ S to export. Escape returns to Select.

Preview limitations: adds content rather than rewriting existing text. No OCR, redaction, form filling, password-protected editing or signature validation yet. Adding a highlight does not remove the text beneath it. Export may invalidate existing digital signatures.
